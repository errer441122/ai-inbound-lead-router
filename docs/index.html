Which customers bring the revenue, and which CRM action does each group need?

RFM segmentation, cohort retention and customer value for a UK online retailer. This is an analysis exercise on a public dataset, not client work.

What the numbers say

  1. Protect Champions before buying reach: 26% of customers, 71% of revenue (VIP & referral flow).
  2. Judge acquisition cost by market: a customer in Germany is worth 48% more over their lifetime than one in United Kingdom.
